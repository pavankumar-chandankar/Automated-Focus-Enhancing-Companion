# Oplyra product strategy (working plan)

## Customer and problem hypotheses
**Initial users:** students, researchers, developers and independent knowledge workers who lose context during interruptions.

**Potential B2B buyers:** operations managers of customer-support and back-office teams who need a more accurate picture of task throughput, rework, workload and process bottlenecks. This B2B demand has **not** been validated.

**Non-goal:** maximizing clicks, mouse movement, keystrokes or time at a desk; these are poor proxies for quality and impact.

## Two-sided product model
1. **Oplyra Desktop:** personal goal, next concrete action, start/pause/resume, checkpoint recovery, personal feedback; optional consent-gated coaching later. A usable offline mode is mandatory.
2. **Oplyra Teams (later):** organization-managed workflow integrations, task counts and cycle-time distributions, rework and quality measures where relevant, capacity/bottleneck trends. Employees can understand what is collected and what is visible to managers.
3. **Oplyra Intelligence (later):** evidence-linked workflow suggestions; never automated discipline or individual employment decisions.

## Validation sequence
- Research: 10 employee interviews + 10 operations-manager interviews; record workflows, current tools, unmet needs and privacy concerns.
- Desktop beta: 20-30 testers. Measure reliable task resumption, usability, app crashes, opt-in retention and the number of voluntarily completed sessions.
- Product signal: 100 recurring users, not downloads; compare contextual resumption cues with a plain timer.
- B2B pilot: up to 3 organizations with a specific process and known quality measures; define baseline, evaluate quality-adjusted throughput and unintended consequences.
- Commercial signal: paid pilots or clear procurement commitments, repeatable deployment, acceptable gross margin and ongoing retention.

Numbers are **targets, not achieved results**. A market-size or investor return claim must be based on independently sourced data.

## Commercial model to test
Free local-first desktop tool. Optional subscription for managed synchronization and supported AI models. For organizations, per-active-seat or per-team plans with minimums, deployment support, identity integration and audited access controls. Pricing should be validated through buyer interviews; no pricing is committed.

## Risks
- False productivity inference: prefer work-output and quality measures to presence/activity signals.
- Employee trust and legal compliance: transparent purpose limitation, human review, no covert monitoring.
- Inference cost: deterministic local fallbacks and model-agnostic adapters.
- Distribution: validate channels before scaling development or fundraising.
- IP and naming: check coauthor/commercial rights and Oplyra trademarks/domains first.

## Go / no-go
If people don't return voluntarily or organizations can't define ethically measurable outcomes, simplify or change the product. Do not add invasive monitoring to manufacture engagement or investor appeal.

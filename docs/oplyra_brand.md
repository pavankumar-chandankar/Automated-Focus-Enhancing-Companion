# Oplyra — working brand

**Status:** selected as a working product name, **not legally cleared**. Do not claim domain, trademark, incorporation or exclusive brand rights.

**Positioning:** Work intelligence, reimagined.

**Company ambition:** An open-source-first software company focused on helping people perform meaningful work and helping organizations improve documented workflows without secret surveillance.

## Product names
- **Oplyra Desktop:** local-first individual companion
- **Oplyra Teams:** planned B2B workflow and capacity dashboards
- **Oplyra Intelligence:** planned explainable workflow suggestions

## Messaging rules
- Say "in development" for the desktop app until installer builds and OS tests pass.
- Say "planned" for Teams/Intelligence, never claim shipped capabilities.
- Avoid claims to read minds, infer emotions, monitor workers invisibly or guarantee productivity gains.
- Existing MayDay (2024) research and its coauthors retain explicit attribution in this repository.
- Before commercialization: check trademark registries, domain availability, company naming, original project contributor agreements and applicable university IP policies.
